How it goes

  1. Browse the catalogue

    Fifty recipes with times, tags and ingredients. Search hits the ingredients too, so "chickpea" finds the curry.

  2. Drop them on a date

    Breakfast, lunch, dinner on real dates — one day on screen at a time, so a week never turns into a spreadsheet.

  3. Come back to it

    Saved in your own browser. Close the tab, open it next week, your plan and your bookmarks are still there.
